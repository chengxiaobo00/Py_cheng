## Definition — clear, precise

Professionalism is an aspect of personal behavior that reflects a person's alignment with the standards and expectations set by their field or role.

It represents certain qualities and behaviors exhibited in work, communication, relationships, decision-making processes, and daily interactions. Professionalism manifests itself through integrity, reliability, respect, empathy, expertise, responsibility, ethical conduct, professionalism towards colleagues and subordinates, upholding a commitment to excellence, and adherence to established norms in one's field of expertise.

## How it works — technical explanation

Professionalism arises when an individual aligns their actions with recognized professional standards. There are multiple factors contributing to the development of a person’s sense of professional behavior:

1. **Education and Training**: Formal education and training programs provide knowledge necessary for developing professional skills.
2. **Work Experience**: Direct experience in one's job role builds expertise, decision-making abilities, and understanding of best practices within the field.
3. **Industry Certification and Licensure**: Certifications and licenses demonstrate adherence to industry standards and may inform professional behaviors.
4. **Leadership in Organizations**: Leadership positions offer opportunities for guiding people, managing teams, negotiating with stakeholders, and demonstrating influence over various aspects.
5. **Networking and Mentorship**: Positive interactions with peers through networking, mentorship, and role models help develop ethical behavior and effective communication skills.

A comprehensive understanding of these elements leads to a well-rounded professional personality and higher performance in one's respective field or business area.

## Variants — known variants if any

Professionalism can be seen as overlapping concepts and manifestations, such as:

- **Ethics**: A principle-driven set of guidelines.
- **Manners/morals**: Etiquette or conduct that ensures social respect and appropriateness.
- **Compliance with the Code of Conduct (CoC)/Standards/Ethos**: Observable adherence to organizational norms.
