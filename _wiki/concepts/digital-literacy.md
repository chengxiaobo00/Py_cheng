# Digital Literacy

## Definition

Digital literacy refers to the ability of an individual or group to access, interpret, evaluate and use digital information and technology effectively to achieve a specific goal. It encompasses a range of skills and knowledge necessary for participation in the digital age.

### How it Works

1. **Accessability:** Knowing how to navigate through digital networks (like the internet) is crucial.
2. **Critical Thinking:** Categorizing, judging information's validity or appropriateness involves understanding credible sources vs fabricated data.
3. **Technological Competence:** Ability to utilize and adapt digital technology according to specific contexts.

### Variants

1. **Offline Literacy:** The idea that people without access to the internet or using it correctly could achieve an equal level of literacy.
2. **Immersed Literacy:** Reflecting on how information is represented in digital forms, not merely its technical aspects.
