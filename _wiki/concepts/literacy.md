## # Definition
Literacy is the ability to read or write and also includes the use of a computer. It is an important skill in our society that allows individuals to communicate effectively, access education and information, work towards progress in society.

## How it works
The concept of literacy can be understood in different ways depending on who you ask:

### Written Language:
Literacy refers specifically to the ability to read and write using language as a symbolic system. This includes understanding sentence structures, recognizing individual words, translating meaning across languages, and interpreting literature for enjoyment and learning.

### Reading:
Reading is an act that involves perceiving and decoding printed text or visual symbols into sounds in one's mind. It provides access not only to textual content but also the ability to comprehend abstract concepts through indirect references when the full context of a document isn't available visually.
  

### Computer Literacy:
Computer literacy encompasses skills in navigating, using, and utilizing computers for both learning materials and tasks. It includes understanding how technology works and being able to effectively use it (e.g., typing on a keyboard).

As literacy is not limited to written or printed texts but also involves the use of computers, we can describe literacy as:
**Literacy = Ability [to read/written/understand languages | to comprehend abstract concepts through indirect references [and interpret literature]] using symbols**

### Variants 
- **Written Literacy**: refers typically to literacy that includes ability in reading and writing.
- **Technical Literacy**: focuses on the skills required for technical use of hardware, software, or machinery.  
- **Digital Literacy**: involves understanding how technology works and being able to effectively use it.
