## Definition — Clear, Precise  
A **presentation** is a **medium** for conveying information to an audience. It utilizes visuals like slideshows and may include audio or video content.

Slides often present text using bullet points with images and diagrams.[[slides]](presentations#variants)

Presentations are used in various settings including educational, workplace, communication purposes, and entertainment contexts.

## How it Works — Technical Explanation  
- **Start from a concept**: Begin by defining the topic clearly and concisely.
- **Plan**: Outline the format and main points before starting to ensure you cover everything necessary.
- **Practice**: Rehearse your slides to have them flow logically and without errors.
- **Engage with audience**: Use gestures, eye contact, and active listening techniques to connect with the audience effectively.

## Variants — Known Variants if any  
- A presentation can be digital (like a PowerPoint or Google Slides), traditional on paper, or interactive with physical demonstrations.  
- Presentations may incorporate audio tracks to enhance understanding while speaking.
