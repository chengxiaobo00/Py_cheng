## Definition 

Creative solutions are unique and effective problem-solving strategies that do not follow conventional methods, but instead devise unconventional or innovative approaches to addressing challenges.

### How it Works

1. **Insight Generation**: Creative solutions often require deep cognitive processes like brainstorming, idea generation, and lateral thinking.
2. **Solution Design**: The process involves breaking down the challenge into its components, then combining these elements in creative combinations that may not immediately appear intuitive or straightforward based on conventional methods.
3. **Implementation Plan**: Creative strategies need clear implementation plans to ensure they are adaptable to new contexts and can be swiftly implemented.

### Variants

- **Adaptation of Existing Ideas (Modification)**: A company might adapt popular social media trends into an employee wellness program with creative themes and activities.
- **Utilization of Unexpected Contexts (Reapplication)**: For instance, organizing a charity event around a specific theme that resonates in a context not typically associated with charitable events.
- **Integration of Technology Not Frequently Used for Problem Solving**: Employing remote working tools as a solution to physical office space limitations or for managing time efficiency.
